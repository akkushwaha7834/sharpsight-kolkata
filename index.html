Retina Treatment

The retina is the most sensitive part of the eye, and treating it requires skilled and experienced specialists. At Sharp Sight Eye Hospital, Kolkata, our retina surgeons have extensive expertise in diagnosing and managing a wide range of retinal conditions. Trust our experienced eye doctors to provide personalized care and advanced treatment for all types of retinal diseases, ensuring the best possible outcomes for your vision.

Cornea Treatment

The cornea plays a key role in focusing light and ensuring clear vision. Damage from injury or infection can lead to serious eyesight issues if left untreated. Don’t delay—early diagnosis and treatment by expert eye care specialists is essential to safeguard your vision.

Glaucoma Surgery

Glaucoma, or Kala Motia, is a silent eye disease that often shows no early symptoms and can gradually cause permanent vision loss. At Sharp Sight Eye Hospital, our experienced glaucoma specialists offer advanced diagnosis and effective treatment. Follow your doctor’s advice and seek timely care to protect your eyesight.

Pediatric Ophthalmology

A detailed eye examination for children often includes dilating the eyes to thoroughly evaluate their eye health. To protect your child from vision problems like Squint (Strabismus) and Amblyopia (Lazy Eye), it’s important to identify and treat these conditions early for the best visual outcomes.

Oculoplasty Surgery

Oculoplasty, also known as Ophthalmic Plastic Surgery, is a specialized procedure involving the eye and its surrounding structures, including the eyelids, tear ducts, and eye socket. It is performed to improve or restore the eye’s function, comfort, and appearance.

OPTICALS

At Sharp Sight Eye Hospital, we offer a large selection of high-quality spectacles to suit every vision need. Our experienced optometrists perform detailed eye examination and provide accurate eyewear solutions for correcting refractive errors such as myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism.
